The diagnosis behind v0.12 (verify it against the data and the game code before judging the fixes):
1. Planet material is a fixed pool (350k). PlayerResourceHandler.TurnStartInit drains max(planet cost, production) a turn, so yield multipliers only spend it faster. Both AIs ran dry at T26-27 (the human at T38), spent the last material on ~180 Floating Plants each while energy went unspent, then could not build or rebuild anything (30-135 material at construction from T28).
2. The native trajectory search (AIWeaponTargeting.Update, AICore.Update m_timeOut = 20 s, step allowance clamp(1200 x AIs / total AI weapons, 30, 100)) runs only during the human's turn; 537 of 2,568 weapons (21%) found a primary trajectory over the match, mostly 6-15 of ~50 at the human, 14-32 at the other AI.
3. The v0.11 abort gate (30% of weapons on target) called off 13 of 26 strikes.
4. ReachabilityMemory marked a target unreachable for every weapon that found no trajectory, so after aiming at X most weapons excluded X next turn and the commit flipped almost every turn.

v0.12 changes:
A. AI/Brain/Director.cs: Weights gives energy gains GlutEnergyGainWeight (0) in energy glut OR energy saturation (checked before material glut). EnergySaturated: only when the planet binds (SustainableMaterialIncome = planet left / max(3, 45 - turn) < material income) and energy income >= 1.5 x (0.6 x min(material income, sustainable) + weapon upkeep). EconomyReserve: 15k material the economy (Best) may not spend once the planet runs dry within BankWarningTurns 8.
B. AI/DirectorPatches.cs: SellDryMines (planet at 0: sell up to 10 non-power mines a turn via PlayerBuildingHandler.SellBuilding(b, true), proceeds added to core.Resources and AllottedMaterial at BeginTurn); MaxWeapons 30 aimed weapons (not lasers, not superweapons) in TryWeapon.
C. AI/Brain/Strategy.cs + StrategyPatches.cs: abort only below max(AbortBelowWeapons 3, AbortBelowShare 0 x weapons); config 0.3 migrates to 0. SetAimWindow writes AICore.m_timeOut: 40 s on strike turns, 20 s on hold turns (also 40 s at AICore.Initialize when the mod is active).
D. AI/Brain/Memory.cs ReportTurn + AI/Patches.cs postfix: a failure is recorded only when at least ReachFailMinShare 0.5 of the weapons aimed at that target found a trajectory; the fire log counts live (Normal-state) weapons only.
