**Major (should be fixed before the record relies on lane A):**

2. C4: the anchor-limited explanation for placement failures is one AI plus Eddie (32% of give-ups); do not extend it to the pool.

**Minor:** the latch is bundled with instrumentation (split it); "35" for the cap is unmotivated; the opening narration lines before the title are

## 5. Disclosures that change lane E's sealed report (the file is unchanged)

Reviewing lane A turned up three defects in `laneE.md`. I have not edited it; the facilitator should apply them.
1. **O1 and R2 overstate the cap.** I classified idle turns as "at cap" first and "material" second, which hid the overlap. Cross-tab of the 52 idle-energy war turns (`matcross.py`): at cap and material < 2,000: 21; below cap and material < 2,000: 18; at cap with material available: 12; below cap with material available: 1. So material is short on 39 of 52 (75%), and the cap alone explains 12 (23%). R2 should be conditioned on `materialLeft` above a floor, or paired with a material diagnosis; its gate G2 baseline should read "12 of 193 war turns", not "33 of 193".
2. **O6's "88.1 deg for all live guns" mixes populations.** I weighted per-turn angle means by `live - found`, but the angle means cover all usable weapons with targets (lane A's caveat, verified at `AimWatch.cs:151-165, 220-254`); 60 of 99 fire turns had off-commit weapons. The unweighted mean of per-turn means is 81.9 deg found / 104.0 unfound, in line with my figures, but the "overall 88 deg equals chance" statement is approximate.
3. **O7's "defense cover searches" are anti-nuke searches only**, and `unplaced` (74% pooled) is not proof of a full planet; the per-AI split above shows it is a mix. Also abandoned/queued is not a complete failure rate (C9).

## 6. Verdict

**verdict:** lane A's evidence can be trusted (11 of 11 checked numbers and mechanisms reproduced; it found the war-latch mechanism, the OA-plus-income gate, the secondary-fill fire and the m23/m25/m26 note errors), but its ranking cannot: move facing to v0.21 behind per-gun data, split the latch from the instrumentation, hold rec 4 to the accuracy rule, and restate the cap/material finding as "material binds on 39 of 52 idle turns".
