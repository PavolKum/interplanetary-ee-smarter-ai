Before: FreeEnergy = min(lastUnspent, energyNowNet + firingCost - lastUses), which equals min(lastUnspent, lastUnspent + growth), so this turn's income growth never counted and a jump in income held fire a turn (match 24: Doc T23 free 1,034 vs firing cost 3,500 with 17,120 energy left at the end of the turn).
