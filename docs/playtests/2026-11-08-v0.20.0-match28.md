## How it went

- **Economy race.** The human led material from T4 (1,994 per turn at T8, 9,082 at T20, 17,879 at T22). Ash and Jeo
  were material-bound from T7 and ran identical builds until T12.
- **The latch fired as designed.** Ash took 2,078 damage at T16 and latched; it returned fire on Boomer (T19-T20), then
  turned HUMAN-FIRST at T22. Jeo latched at T19, opened on Boomer at T20, and went HUMAN-FIRST from T21.
- **The hard stretch for the human was T22-T24.** Both Smarter AIs fired late volleys at the human on consecutive
  turns. Population fell from 2,760 (T22) to 1,843 (T24), 33% off its peak. Jeo bought railgun population damage at T23
  and T25.
- **Recovery.** Human energy rose to 30k (T24), 37.7k (T25) and 53.9k (T41). Population recovered from T25 and
  peaked at 4,809 (T44). Human material income hit 0 from T34 (crust exhausted).
- **Collapse.** Ash fell from 131 buildings (T27) to 47 (T41); Jeo from 195 (T32) to 153 (T42). Jeo queued nuclear
  missiles at T31 and T42. Both were eliminated in the T43 action phase.
