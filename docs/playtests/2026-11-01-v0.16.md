## What v0.16 did, by council item (decision-record-r2 section 3)

### 1. Slot engine
**Partly worked. One design gap.**
- **Opening cap held:** two Molecular Recycling Plants (T4, T5), then no more permanents.
- **EMC completed at T13** and unlocked both Jamming and Rare Material Synthesis.
- **The scorer then put all 5 free slots into Rare Material Synthesis** (material x1.3, energy x0.95, planet cost x1.3, 6 turns) instead of Jamming. It was material-starved: 58 material left against 1,150 energy idle.
  - **Material:** went from 3,276 to 12,513 per turn.
  - **Science:** stayed at 400-600, against Pavol's 1,400-1,700.
  - **SSTAR:** couldn't get a slot until the copies expired at T19-T20.
- **The gap:** the reservation protects slots only from permanent projects, not from other temporaries.
- **Gates:**
  - 3 Jammings within 2 turns of EMC: FAIL (first Jamming at T16; 3 at T20).
  - 3 SSTARs by T20: FAIL (2 at T21, 3 at T24).
  - 15,000 energy at T20: FAIL (9,065), but 32.7k at T22 and **52.0k at T24, against Pavol's 31.1k**.

### 2. Research
**Order executed exactly; the order itself was the problem.**
- **Timing:** EMC head at T9 and done at T13, both gates PASS. No `research-guard` event all match (zero hijack).
- **But the economy-first order put the defense and intel techs too late for 400-600 science per turn:**
  - Microwave done by T16; Lossless T18.
  - Telescope tech (Optics) head at T18; first Telescopes T19.
  - Maglev (Kinetic) T20.
  - Plasma T21: the gate was T18, FAIL.
  - Orbital Assembly and Early Warning AI T22.
- **The stall, T16-T19:** the AI bought nothing on T17-T18 and almost nothing on T16 and T19, with 6,500-7,000 energy idle each turn and 24-61k material banked. Every useful purchase was locked behind research: Kinetic, Telescopes, weapons. The Science Institute (permanent) was also blocked by the opening cap.

### 3. Energy-to-material target 1.0
**Mixed.**
- The opening was material-bound: 200-675 energy idle on T1-T5, against Pavol's material-heavy start.
- From T13 the material glut ended the shortage.
- At T22 the glut cleared and the AI bought **86 Offshore Mines in one turn**. Together with Rare Material x3 (T24) the planet drained about 50k a turn: 193k at T24, 143k at T25, 72k at T30.

### 4. Offense
**Fair, artillery-only, outfought.**
- **Fair-play rules held:**
  - Blind every turn of the war: `visibleCities` 0, `targetCandidates` 0.
  - No precision fire and no guided purchases, so 0 `fire-refused` (no guided weapons existed).
  - The per-class quotas matched the final lists every strike turn (`fire-plan`).
- **War:** started at the T20 fallback. Orbital Assembly only came at T22, so the early T16 start never triggered. It bought 30 Railguns on T20.
- **Strikes:**
  - First at **T24 (saturated, 21 hits + 2 misses)**, a turn before Pavol's.
  - Then T26 and T27 (attrition) and T30 (3 weapons left).
- **Firing ledger:** 56 paid shots, 7,790 E, all unguided.
- **Blind artillery package:** Triple Barrel at T23, Optimized Airburst at T24 (6 Railguns each).
- **Aiming:** on hold turns only 4-7 of 30 Railguns found a trajectory (20 s window); on strike turns 18-21.
- **Result:** Pavol's population grew every turn (3,539 at T24, 4,182 at T30). The Railgun fire hit buildings, not cities.

### 5. Intel
**Fair, but too late and too timid.**
- The bid opened at T14 but had nothing to buy until the Telescope tech arrived at T18.
- **Batches:** 3 arrays at T19, judged "failed" at T21. One more array and 3 Lidar followed, then a stop-loss at T24.
- **Total intel spend: 1,000 E of a 10% budget worth about 27,000 E.** The AI's intel reached 24-44; Pavol's smallest city needed about 130 (x2.9) at T20 and about 244 (x5.4) at T24.
- **Why it failed:** the batch rule judges a 3-array batch by whether a city became visible. When the gap is 3-5x, no single batch can succeed, so the stop-loss tripped while 96% of the budget sat unused.
- **Fairness:** no hidden value was read. The policy simply never bought enough sight.

## Diagnosis
**v0.16 solved the economy and lost the tempo.**
- For the first time the AI out-produced Pavol on energy through T20: 67.6k against 63.1k.
- But it had half his science (8.0k against 16.9k) and nothing to convert energy into until T20-T22.
- Pavol, at his best-ever opening, used T12-T20 to research, stack Jamming and SSTAR, hide (x2.9-5.4), and build 30 Railguns by T20.
- The AI reached the war phase with a giant economy and 6 Kinetics, blind, with an intel policy that gave up early.

## v0.16.1 candidates (for the review, ranked by evidence)
1. **Science tempo.** Reserve Jamming slots after EMC against all other temporaries (cap Rare Material at 1-2 copies). Exempt the Science Institute from the opening permanent cap. Value science against idle energy: when energy-glutted, science projects and Jamming are the sink.
2. **Research order.** Move Kinetic (2nd Gen + Maglev) and Telescopes (Optics + Advanced Signal Processing) ahead of Nano, Microwave and 4th Gen, so they land around T12-T14, as in match 16 (Kinetic by T15).
3. **Intel bid sizing.** Size a batch to the remaining budget, not a fixed 3. Judge on visibility after a budget-scaled push. Raise the stop-loss threshold so a 1,000 E spend cannot exhaust a 27k budget.
4. **Planet drain guard.** No Rare Material Synthesis and no mass extraction (86 Offshore Mines in a turn) when the projected drain empties the planet before the material horizon (MaterialHorizonTurn 45).
5. **Defense geometry.** Coverage placement for Kinetics against where impacts actually landed (22 of 51 met none).
6. **Deferred items from the Sol review:** Ion site reservation, intel and war state across save/load, the forecast refinements.
