## What lost it

### Placement (Pavol's hypothesis, now with evidence)
Pavol before the war: "unless you will be able to influence position where AI places the buildings, you'll have difficulty
reliably beating me." The match showed placement failing for every building type:
- **Weapons:**
  - At T21, **12 of Eddie's 30 weapons were "unreachable"** from the human's planet; 17 could aim.
  - T22-T23: 5 unreachable each.
  - The 13 Railguns bought at T20 were placed with no trajectory to the target.
- **Power:**
  - T22: 4 Solar and 3 Nuclear Plants abandoned (placement gave up).
  - T24: 1 Solar; T25: 4 Solar; T26: 1 Solar.
- **Defenses:**
  - T18-T20: Ion Defense ×1-2 and Kinetic Defense ×2 abandoned.
  - Cover searches: 5 of 9 gave up (Eddie); 4 of 4 placed (Junker, T25-T26).
- **The human's layout (Pavol, after the match):**
  - "Cities are spawned randomly... I don't control that."
  - "I cover everything with defenses... there is like no gap anywhere."
  - "Cities are under like [a cloverleaf] array of defenses."
  - "All the mines and all the water buildings are hugging the shoreline, so they are under defenses too."

### Project timing
Pavol's rule: the material project "decreases your energy... it's all about timing... hit this right like a few turns
before the shooting starts, because then you'll need the energy, then you need to flip to lossless energy transfer."
The AI capped the material project at 1 and did not time it to the war.

### Late war from slow research
- War start was T20 for both AIs from T1: `WarTurnFor` gives T16 only with Orbital Assembly, else `WarFallbackTurn`.
- Eddie finished Orbital Assembly at T18 and started war at T19 (first weapons: 11 Missiles and 8 Railguns).
- Cause: the 2.6x science gap.

## Bug: Junker's turn start failed from T15 to T24
- **Junker's logs, T15-T24:** no `strategy`, `economy` or start-of-turn `director` records.
  - The end-of-turn director logged a stale "T14" record 10 more times.
  - Material piled up 5,241 per turn, to 51.1k at T25, with nothing built.
  - `research` (from `AITechQueue.BeginTurn`) and `build` (end of turn) records continued.
- **Mechanism (decompiled `PlayerData.UpdateAI`):** `AICore.EndTurn` only runs after `AICore.BeginTurn` set
  `Status = TurnOngoing`.
  - So `BeginTurn` was entered every turn and threw inside the module loop, after `AITechQueue`.
  - The candidates are threat assessment, short-term strategy, power grid, intel and construction.
  - A Harmony postfix does not run when the original throws, so the mod's postfix was skipped.
  - The modules after the throw never began their turn, so nothing was built.
- **No stack trace:** the game writes no Player.log, and nothing reached LogOutput.log.
- It recovered at T25, after Junker lost 3 of its 5 cities.
- **Fix candidate:** a Harmony finalizer on each AI module's `BeginTurn` that logs the exception with its stack trace
  once and lets the remaining modules run. That gives a diagnosis next time and keeps the AI alive.

## v0.17 decision (Pavol, 7 Nov)
"placement first, go ahead". Aimed artillery moves to v0.18. Scope:
1. **Placement by the human's rules:**
   - A defense ring over each city before anything else.
   - Every other building inside existing coverage.
   - Water buildings on the shoreline under land defenses.
   - Weapons placed where they have a trajectory to the committed target.
2. **Material project timing:** up to one per city during the build-up, ending a few turns before the war, then SSTAR.
3. **Turn-start resilience:** the module finalizer (bug above).
4. **Open:** the Science Institute miss and research pace (the war start depends on Orbital Assembly).
