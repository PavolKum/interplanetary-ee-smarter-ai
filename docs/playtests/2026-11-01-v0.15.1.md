# Playtest 1 Nov 2026 (fifteenth match) - AI v0.15.1, 1v1 (Thistle, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 39

Same map type (350k planet material), 1v1 from turn 1, live monitor. Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0151-*` (git-ignored).
Mod loaded clean (0.15.1, 15/15 hooks, the v0.15 research plan replaced the old default); the only exception is the
console's CSharpCodeCompiler mono path. The longest match yet (v0.13.1: T30, v0.14.1: T29).

## The human's plan
Pure Railgun pressure from T8: 3 Railguns at T8, 8 at T15, 21 by T26, 37 by T36. Shots per turn 3-5 early, 12-31 at
T19-T24, 19-101 from T28 (T29 20, T32 19, T35 47; T37 101 Railgun + 4 Missile). 4 Kinetic until T24, 10-11 from T29, 5 Ion from T29. Counterintel x1.2 (T17) -> x5.7 (T36)
with 8 Data Security Hubs; a single Telescope (T33). 4 Missiles only at T36.

## Calibration: a different human strategy
Pavol: early fire this time "threw me off the build-out focus that I usually do... much closer than I expected."
Human energy per turn, v0.14.1 vs this match: T12 1,975 vs 1,300; T16 4,780 vs 2,100; T20 8,400 vs 1,775; T25
20,419 vs 4,175. AI at T20: Bolide 17,740 vs Thistle 5,454 (Thistle was hit from T10 and repaired 10-20 buildings a
turn from T18). Early fire shrank both economies, the AI's relatively less (AI/human at T20: 3.1x vs 2.1x). The closer
result is partly the human's weaker build, so it is not clean evidence of AI strength against the usual build-out.

## What v0.15 / v0.15.1 changed, measured
- **Intel sprint worked:** Telescopes at T6 (v0.14.1: T16), all five human cities seen by T9 (v0.14.1: never).
  Counterintel hid three again by T19 and 2-4 late (x4-5.7, intel 112 -> 8). Seen cities going back to Invisible means
  the match most likely ran with SeenBuildingsShadows off (Sol v0.15.2 #7); v0.15.3 logs the setting in `init`.
- **The sprint cost about three turns of energy growth:** 8 Telescopes queued at T6-T8, one refunded, 7 standing at T9 (step 3) left no energy for plants
  (step 6). Energy per turn 925 -> 785 at T9, while v0.14.1 Bolide reached 1,425 at T8. It recovered by T13 (2,185) and
  out-produced the human for most of T13-T31, not all of it (T26 5,105 vs 7,394; T30 8,014 vs 11,618; peak 8,021 at T31). The v0.15.1 material guard never bound:
  material was in glut (stock 4-11k from T12, `soldMaterial` 0). Energy was the scarce resource.
- **Accuracy fix (Sol v0.15 #1) works for new weapons:** `guided` 1-5 once Missiles stood (T22+), `acc` 0.709-0.723.
  But stale entries still counted: pooled building components came back Normal and powered under destroyed weapon
  objects (T32: 27 registered, 33 counted; T38: 6 vs 13). Fixed in v0.15.3 (Sol v0.15.2 #1).
- **Strike planner works on visible targets:** 15 Missile strikes, 8 at cities, 7 at weapon buildings. Cities taken
  weakest first (471 -> 338 -> 193 on one city, T25-T27). All 15 picks were visible; no native fallback.
- **City cover partly worked:** no guided threat (the human had no Missiles until T36), so the prior target of 1 per
  city applied from T24. T26 queued three city-cover Ions and refunded one; from T27 two powered Ions protected two of
  three cities. Every later search for the third city was refunded, so `minCityCover` (the least-covered city) stayed 0
  until T35, when that city fell (Sol v0.15.2 corrections).

## Why it lost (ranked)
1. **It held fire while the human hit it every turn.** T19-T23: 5 turns held with 8-22 Railguns, 80-82% of the salvo
   expected through, short only of `MinPassingShots` 12 (4.5-11.8). Meanwhile the human's 12-27 shots a turn took a
   city (T20) and put another at 20 population (T22). T34-T37: 4 more turns held at 64-70% through (9.8-11.0 shots)
   while its buildings fell 112 -> 67. By T38 its operational weapons fell 22 -> 6 (intact Railguns 21 -> 11; the rest
   disconnected); attrition fired what was left. At T34 the attrition rule missed by 0.05 weapons (27 -> 23, needed 4.05).
2. **Its defenses met few of the human's shots:** 56-85 impacts a turn from T34, 39-63 with no defense in range, with
   21-28 Kinetic standing. Unguided Railguns scatter ±90° around their aim point, so a few defenses cover little.
3. **Science fell behind:** 250-450 a turn against the human's 500-900; the human had 30 techs by T35, 32 at T38.
5. **Material collapse late (Sol v0.15.2 #5):** material income 4,317 (T30) -> 197 (T37) with 212k still in the planet;
   repairs and reconnection plants took the material before new extraction, leaving 2-3k energy idle. Deferred to v0.16.
4. **Energy spent on Telescopes in the opening** (see above); recovered, but the human's late economy (16.6k energy at
   T37) out-grew it once the volleys started landing.

## v0.15.2 (built after this match)
- **Return fire:** under attack, fire when at least 60% of the salvo is expected through and at least 4 shots (pulse or
  not). v0.15.3: the signal is enemy impacts in the previous action phase (v0.15.2 used the decaying grudge, which
  would also fire at T19 and keep one hit alive all match), and the firing-time abort applies. On this match's held
  turns it qualifies at T20-T23, T31 and T34-T37 (a predicate check on recorded states, not a replay).
- **City-cover search on the city's ring:** candidates are drawn on the ring around a city still below the target
  (clear of the city, inside the protecting reach) and connected to the nearest anchor in line reach. A `cover-search`
  record logs tries and why each failed (invalid, unplaced, not closing, no anchor).
- **Intel energy guard:** intel wanted only to find unseen cities waits while energy is below 450 (a Telescope plus a
  power plant), so the economy step still gets a plant that turn.
