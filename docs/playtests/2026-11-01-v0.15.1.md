# Playtest 1 Nov 2026 (fifteenth match) - AI v0.15.1, 1v1 (Thistle, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 39

Same map type (350k planet material), 1v1 from turn 1, live monitor. Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0151-*` (git-ignored).
Mod loaded clean (0.15.1, 15/15 hooks, the v0.15 research plan replaced the old default); the only exception is the
console's CSharpCodeCompiler mono path. The longest match yet (v0.13.1: T30, v0.14.1: T29).

## The human's plan
Pure Railgun pressure from T8: 3 Railguns at T8, 8 at T15, 21 by T26, 37 by T36. Shots per turn 3-5 early, 12-31 at
T19-T24, 53-101 from T28. 4 Kinetic until T24, 10-11 from T29, 5 Ion from T29. Counterintel x1.2 (T17) -> x5.7 (T36)
with 8 Data Security Hubs; a single Telescope (T33). 4 Missiles only at T36.

## What v0.15 / v0.15.1 changed, measured
- **Intel sprint worked:** Telescopes at T6 (v0.14.1: T16), all five human cities seen by T9 (v0.14.1: never).
  Late in the game the human's counterintel (x4-5.7) and lost Telescopes (intel 112 -> 8) hid 2-4 cities again.
- **The sprint cost about three turns of energy growth:** 8 Telescopes at T6-T8 (step 3) left no energy for plants
  (step 6). Energy per turn 925 -> 785 at T9, while v0.14.1 Bolide reached 1,425 at T8. It recovered by T13 (2,185) and
  out-produced the human from T13 to T31 (peak 8,021 at T30-T31; human 6,729). The v0.15.1 material guard never bound:
  material was in glut (stock 4-11k from T12, `soldMaterial` 0). Energy was the scarce resource.
- **Accuracy fix (Sol v0.15 #1) works:** every `accuracy` record counts the live list; `guided` 1-5 once Missiles stood
  (T22+). `acc` 0.709-0.723 = 0.85 for guided, 0.7 for unguided. Dead entries no longer count (shots 38, live 31).
- **Strike planner works on visible targets:** 15 Missile strikes, 8 at cities, 7 at weapon buildings. Cities taken
  weakest first (471 -> 338 -> 193 on one city, T25-T27). No Shadow candidate ever appeared, and no native fallback.
- **City cover never happened:** no guided threat (the human had no Missiles until T36), so the prior target of 1 per
  city applied from T24. Every Ion search from T26 to T33 was refunded by the v0.15.1 rule (placement must close a city
  deficit). `minCityCover` stayed 0 until T35. Two Ions stood from T27, built elsewhere by the counter-defense step.

## Why it lost (ranked)
1. **It held fire while the human hit it every turn.** T19-T23: 5 turns held with 8-22 Railguns, 80-82% of the salvo
   expected through, short only of `MinPassingShots` 12 (4.5-11.8). Meanwhile the human's 12-27 shots a turn took a
   city (T20) and put another at 20 population (T22). T34-T37: 4 more turns held at 64-70% through (9.8-11.0 shots)
   while its buildings fell 112 -> 67. At T38 the human destroyed 16 of its 22 Railguns unfired; attrition fired the
   last 6. At T34 the attrition rule missed by 0.05 weapons (27 -> 23, needed 4.05).
2. **Its defenses met few of the human's shots:** 56-85 impacts a turn from T34, 39-63 with no defense in range, with
   21-28 Kinetic standing. Unguided Railguns scatter ±90° around their aim point, so a few defenses cover little.
3. **Science fell behind:** 250-450 a turn against the human's 500-900; the human had 30 techs by T38.
4. **Energy spent on Telescopes in the opening** (see above); recovered, but the human's late economy (16.6k energy at
   T37) out-grew it once the volleys started landing.

## v0.15.2 (built after this match)
- **Return fire:** under attack, fire when at least 60% of the salvo is expected through and at least 4 shots (pulse or
  not, exempt from the abort). Replayed on this match's held turns (impacts from the previous action phase as the
  attack signal) it fires at T20-T23, T31 and T34-T37; T19 followed an action phase without impacts.
- **City-cover search on the city's ring:** candidates are drawn on the ring around a city still below the target
  (clear of the city, inside the protecting reach) and connected to the nearest anchor in line reach. A `cover-search`
  record logs tries and why each failed (invalid, unplaced, not closing, no anchor).
- **Intel energy guard:** intel wanted only to find unseen cities waits while energy is below 450 (a Telescope plus a
  power plant), so the economy step still gets a plant that turn.
