## Why it still lost: a 277-building economy with no defenses
The human used **Railguns only** (24 at T27, 38 at T33; 40-80 shots/turn from T28) and destroyed the AI's buildings:
27,504 building HP on T28, 22,269 on T31. Fey went 279 buildings (T28) -> 200 -> 157 -> 64 (T32) -> 19.

1. **Defenses sized from visible enemy weapons only, and the human blinded the AI right before arming.**
   `desiredDefenses` was 0 through T22 (the human had 0 weapons while Fey could see him, T11-T20). From T21 the
   human's counterintel (7 Data Security Hubs, multiplier 2.05 at T20 -> 8.76 at T27) beat Fey's 10-13 telescopes:
   `blind 1` for most of T21-T37. The human built 24 Railguns in that window; Fey counted 0. The first defenses
   came from the damage term (T23: 3 wanted, T24: 16, T27: 40), then `DefensesPerTurn 3` limited the build to
   3/turn: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 ... 20 at T31, while it lost 100+ buildings a turn. Human for comparison: 19 Kinetic
   Defenses at T20, before he had any weapon, 22 Kinetic + 7 Ion at T27.
   - Blind was read as "no threat". The defensive half of the director has no prior: no floor scaled to what we
     own, no memory of the last seen weapon count, no assumption from the enemy's tech/turn.
2. **The AI's offense hit a wall of defenses.** Fey had more weapons (41 Railguns + 16 Missiles at T26 vs the
   human's 24 Railguns), 435 shots logged as hits, but the human's 22-29 defenses plus 7-11k leftover energy per
   turn absorbed them: human population dipped only at T21 (-615) and T27 (-145), and he never lost a building
   count. The AI kept firing kinetic weapons into kinetic defenses.
3. **Planet burned in 20 turns.** Planet material 288k (T10) -> 218k (T20) -> 19k (T30) -> 0 (T37); drain 22-25k/turn
   at peak. The human still had 88k at T33. Even without the attack, Fey's material income would have ended
   around T31. The material became buildings that were then destroyed, not weapons or defenses.
4. **Superweapon too late.** Nuclear Missile research queued T27 (bank from T27); one Nuclear Missile building
   existed at T37, after the collapse. No evidence it fired.
5. Lost the intel race: counterintel x8.8 vs ~10 telescopes. MaxIntelBuildings 30 / IntelPerTurn 2 were not the
   limit (it had ~10-13); the director stopped adding telescopes once blind returned (to check).

## v0.5 plan (ranked, before Terra's diagnosis review)
1. **Defense floor not tied to visibility**: from war start, defenses scale with what we own (buildings/cities) and
   the enemy's last known or estimated arsenal; remember the last seen weapon count (decay, not reset) while blind;
   treat blindness after sight as a warning sign (enemy built counterintel = enemy is hiding something).
2. **DefensesPerTurn scales with income** (3/turn is nothing at 25k E/t), and defenses before more economy once
   damage arrives (damage should preempt the economy step entirely).
3. **Mixed defense types** sized to the enemy's weapon mix (Kinetic vs Railgun, Ion vs Missiles) - check DefensePlanner
   gets the remembered mix while blind.
4. **Offense vs defended targets**: if hit rate on an enemy collapses (shots absorbed), shift to what defenses don't
   stop (Missiles vs Kinetic, Ion-bypassing weapons, lasers) or to volleys that exhaust the defender's energy; push
   the superweapon earlier when the AI has a 2x economy.
5. **Planet pacing**: when drain would empty the planet in < N turns, prefer refinery/efficiency and energy over
   more mines; keep material for rebuilding.
6. Intel arms race: when blind returns after sight, keep adding telescopes up to the cap (check why it stalled at 10-13).
