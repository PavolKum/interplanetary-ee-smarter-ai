# Playtest 8 Nov 2026 (twenty-sixth match) - AI v0.19.0: Ash and Hawking (Smarter AI, Impossible) vs Armiger (original, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 44

This was the rematch after match 25, with Pavol back on his standard play. The lobby is not identical: two Smarter AIs,
not three. The crust was 400,000. Claude monitored it live from T1.

The game was not restarted after match 25, so both matches share `decisions-20261108-111328.jsonl`.
`v019m26-ai-decisions.jsonl` keeps only Ash, Hawking and Armiger.

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v019m26-*` (git-ignored). Mod 0.19.0.

- **Eliminations (last record):** Hawking T35, Armiger T37, Ash T43. Human victory at T44 (telemetry `Victorious`).
- **Peak energy per turn:** Ash 52.1k (T35), Hawking 50.4k (T22); the human 46.5k (T40).

## How it went

- **War opened late again:** Ash at T17, Hawking at T20. Both built economy, defenses and telescopes first.
- **Hawking was the threat early.**
  - It went HUMAN-FIRST at T21, used late free fire that turn, then queued 20 railguns.
  - A global energy project took it to 50k energy a turn; 36-39k of it went unspent at the weapon cap.
  - It fired at the human every turn T22-T29: 13 volleys, 174 planned hits.
- **The human dipped once:** population 3,009 to 1,966 at T23-T24, the only real damage. He recovered to 3,028 by T30
  and 5,000 by T43. He never lost a building (63 at T20, 231 at T43).
- **Ash split its fire for the first half.** It shot at Armiger (T22, T24) and at Hawking (T25), and only went
  HUMAN-FIRST from T23/T26 to the end: 14 volleys, 184 planned hits. Its aim at the human was often poor (7-13 of 14-23
  guns, the unaimed ones 103-157 degrees away).
- **The human ground them down:**
  - Hawking repaired 18-26 buildings a turn from T26 and fell from 176 buildings (T26) to 91 (T35).
  - Ash fell from 303 (T37) to 48 (T43).
