# Playtest 1 Nov 2026 (thirteenth match) - AI v0.13.1, 1v1 (Guts, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 30

New map (300k planet material each), 1v1, from turn 1. Live monitor all match. Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0131-*`
(AI decisions, human telemetry, BepInEx log; git-ignored). Per-turn human summary: `python AI/tools/human_summary.py
docs/playtests/data/v0131-human-telemetry.jsonl`; turn timing and early builds: `AI/tools/human_turns.py`. Not comparable
turn-for-turn with the 2-AI matches; judged on mechanisms.

## What worked (first time in a real game)
- **Repair**: 11-34 buildings repaired per turn from T21 (T24: 34 for 3.4k energy, 5.3k material).
- **Material glut switch**: T17, 11.9k idle material -> no Mines, 12 Nuclear Plants queued; energy 4.6k -> 8.0k a turn.
- **Missiles by T22** (Orbital Assembly was the research target from T18; v0.12.1: all 30 weapons were Railguns).
- **Hidden weapons counted from impacts**: `enemyWeapons` estimated 40-49 Railguns (the human really had 40) with
  `visibleWeapons` empty all match.
- One enemy, so every strike committed to the human; on hold turns aiming was 11/11 and 17/17.
- Mod loaded clean: 0.13.1, 15 of 15 hooks; the one exception in the log is the console's CSharpCodeCompiler mono path.

## Why it lost (ranked)
1. **Defenses: too few, too late, outside the hits.** Interception mechanics (decompiled `PlayerDefenseHandler`): every
   Normal, powered defense within 5.9 of the impact rolls in turn (cheapest first) until one succeeds; no per-defense
   limit; each roll costs 100 energy (Kinetic) hit or miss. The human fired 100-120 Railgun projectiles a turn from T22
   (40 Railguns x 3 projectiles). Guts had 18-21 Kinetic, desired 19-27 at T20-T23 (floor 0.15 x non-city buildings; no
   visible enemy weapons) and built at the 3-a-turn base rate while 13-28k material sat idle. 70-80% of impacts met no
   defense in range (T25: 98 impacts, 27 intercepted, 68 with none nearby); where one was in range ~90% were stopped. The
   human held 23 Kinetic per ~100 non-city buildings before firing. Railgun AoE 6.0 ~ Kinetic reach 5.9, so a few leaks
   near a defense can remove it (Kinetic 300 HP vs Railgun 125 x2 with the damage upgrade); Guts's defenses fell 21 -> 12.
   Coverage-aware placement exists since v0.7; quantity and timing were the limit this match.
2. **Economy and research gap.** Human: Microwave Energy Transmission + Nanostructuring T13, Robotic Mining T14 (Mine
   and Offshore yield x2 on every existing mine), Magnetic Radiation Shielding T15 -> energy 3.8k -> 8.0k and material
   8.3k -> 13.1k in one turn (T16). Guts: Nanostructuring T19, Microwave T20, Magnetic Radiation Shielding T22, Robotic
   Mining never (#25 in the plan); 18 techs vs 30, science ~600 vs ~1,200 a turn from T12.
3. **Opening.** T1: 0 free power slots, so no Mine could be queued (queued plants did not count their connectors) and the
   economy bought 3 Solar Plants with 1,200 of 1,350 material; 750-975 energy unspent each turn T2-T6. Human: 1 Solar +
   1 Mine at T1, 3 Mines by T2.
4. **Weak strikes, then held fire while being destroyed.** Two strikes (T23, T24): 6/26 and 9/26 weapons found a
   trajectory. AIWeaponTargeting gives each AI weapon clamp(1200 / all AI weapons, 30, 100) search steps per frame: 100 at
   <= 12 weapons, 46 at 26. Human turn length also matters (T28: 20.6 s, 3/10 aimed). Then Guts held at 26 -> 17 -> 11
   -> 10 live weapons (T24-T27) waiting for a saturating salvo.
5. **Blind all match.** The human's 7 Data Security Hubs (counterintel x3.85-7.6) beat 9 Telescope Arrays. Guts's own
   Data Security Hub came at T23 (it needs Magnetic Radiation Shielding).
6. Placement retries: 6 Offshore Mines abandoned at T14, 14 queued and 13 abandoned at T18; power plants T25-T27 (ruins
   likely; refunds made it cheap).

Not a cause (corrected during the match): "material glut after income collapsed" - at T27 energy (4.7k a turn) bound,
14.6k material was 3 turns of spending; the glut test (stock vs energy budget) was right.
