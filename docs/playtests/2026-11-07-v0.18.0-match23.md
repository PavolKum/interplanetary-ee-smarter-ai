# Playtest 7 Nov 2026 (twenty-third match) - AI v0.18.0: Beastie and Arc (Smarter AI, Impossible) vs Spiral (original, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 38

This was the first lobby with two Smarter AIs. The crust was 500,000. Claude watched T1-T10, then handed over to Codex
(GPT) for the rest of the match because of the usage limit. Codex's handoff is in
`docs/playtests/data/v018m23-codex-handoff.md`.

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v018m23-*` (git-ignored). Mod 0.18.0, 16/16 hooks, turn guard 9/9, aim watch 1/1.
`v018m23-monitor.log` also holds match 22 at the top; match 23 starts at `decisions-20261107-204932`.

- **Eliminations (last record):** Beastie T34, Arc T35, Spiral T37. Human victory at T38 (telemetry `Victorious` and the
  victory screen).
- **Peak energy per turn:** Beastie 34.6k at T24 and Arc 27.9k at T24; the human 27.3k at T32. **This is the first
  match in which an AI out-produced the human at the peak.**

## Two Eiais did real damage

- **Identical play to T10**, then small drifts. Both went to war: Arc at T16, Beastie at T20.
- **Both picked the human:** HUMAN-FIRST for Arc T20-T35, Beastie T21-T34.
- **16 volleys at the human:** Beastie 8 (103 hits, 18 misses), Arc 8 (155 hits, 42 misses). Firing turns aimed most
  guns (for example Arc T23 24/25, T28 24/24; Beastie T28 23/25, T33 24/25).
- **The human lost a city.** Population fell from 3,059 to 2,353 at T22 and slid from 2,608 to 2,147 over T30-T34. Pavol
  confirmed the city loss. The telemetry still listed 5 cities, so the city count there cannot be trusted.
- **Buildings kept rising** (95 at T22 to 254 at T34), so the damage hit population, not the build-up.

## What lost it

- **Not enough, not early enough.** About 700M population was the most damage. The human's economy caught up after T26
  and his fire ended both AIs by T35.
- **Unaimed guns face away from the target:** 108 degrees on average (29 samples). Beastie aimed only 6 of 20 at T31.
- **Placement gave up** 59 times: IonDefense 32 (Arc 27), SolarPlant 14, NuclearPlant 13, TelescopeArray 5.
- **Fire held with energy left over** once: Beastie at T23 held for 132 free against 3,300 firing cost, with 24,335
  left at the end of the turn.

## Aim time is too long

- **Over 90 s on 25 of 69 aim searches,** max 178 s (T33). Most stops were "hold window" with no extension (+0 s), so the
  base window itself runs long; only 6 were extensions that stopped at the cap.
