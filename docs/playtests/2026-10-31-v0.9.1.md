# Playtest 31 Oct 2026 (ninth match) - AI v0.9.1, two AIs (Guts, Knight, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 39

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v091-*.jsonl` (git-ignored), live monitor.

## What v0.9 changed (first time in any match)
- **Science projects bought** (T11: 2x ScienceYieldPermanent1 each); AI science 513 at T12 vs ~405 before (+26%).
- **Weapon upgrades bought** from T16: RailgunPopulationDamage1, RailgunEnergyCostDown1 (dozens per AI), later
  RadiotelescopeScienceYield1 (Guts: 12 in one turn at T29).
- **Guts out-researched the human** from T28: 700 -> 948 science vs the human's 663-687 (+38% at T30).
- **Economy parity to T25**: T22 human 6,077 E/t vs Guts 5,970 / Knight 6,500; AIs ~98-140 buildings vs the human's 85-133.
  Earlier matches had the human at 9-13k E/t by T22.
- More guns for the same energy: 24-25 Railguns at T18 (20-22 before), 33-35 by T23-24.
- Anti-nuke and coverage ran as in v0.8.3 (not re-measured here).

## How the human won
No weapons until T28, 16-19 Kinetic Defenses; then 51 Railguns at once (T28) and a Solar Laser. Energy pulled away from
T28 (9.7k -> 15k -> 18.6k -> 24k) with energy projects; the AIs stayed at 6-7k. Knight lost cities from ~T19 (its science fell
516 -> 113) and collapsed at T29 (144 -> 84 buildings). Guts went 151 -> 76 buildings in one turn at T33 and was eliminated
by T38-39. The human's population dipped at T25, T29 and T36: the AIs' upgraded Railguns landed some hits.

## Regressions / next
1. **Research reorder delayed defenses by one turn**: Maglev Servos (Kinetic Defense) moved 11 -> 12 and Plasma Channel
   Induction (Ion) 13 -> 15 behind Uranium Alloys. Kinetic Defenses arrived T17 instead of T16. Fix: defense techs ahead of
   the weapon-upgrade techs.
2. **Late-game energy gap** remains the decisive one: human 15-24k E/t from T30 vs AI 6-7k. Next: price and buy energy
   projects (EnergyYieldShort / global) the way the human does, and check what the human's spikes are.
3. Science weight ~3.0 from T15 (27,850 science left in the plan): the AIs' energy at T15 was 3,045 vs 4,500-5,300 before;
   recovered by T17. Worth tuning (cap 4 -> 2.5?) once the energy work is in.
4. Guts' 30 Kinetic Defenses did not hold against 51 Railguns at once (T33): look at coverage and reserve at T32-33.
