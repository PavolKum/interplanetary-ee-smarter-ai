# Playtest 1 Nov 2026 (fourteenth match) - AI v0.14.1, 1v1 (Bolide, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 29

New map (350k planet material), 1v1 from turn 1, live monitor. Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0141-*` (git-ignored).
Mod loaded clean (0.14.1, 15/15 hooks); the only exception is the console's CSharpCodeCompiler mono path.

## The strongest AI yet
- **Opening fixed:** T1 one Solar Plant, 950 material kept; T2 two Mines. Material per turn 600 (T4), 1,175 (T6),
  1,650 (T7, human 1,300), 2,951 (T10, human 2,782).
- **Out-produced the human from T7 to the end:** T14 3,125 E / 5,078 M (human 2,895 / 3,877); T20 17,740 E (human
  8,400) after Floating Plant Yield I x47 (T18) and Yield II x48 (T19); peak 28,961 E (T27, human 13,220).
- **Weapon choice against the target worked:** 19 Missiles + 1 Railgun at T20 against a Kinetic-only human.
- **Aiming fixed by the 2600 budget:** 18/20, 9/23, 22/23, 23/23, 23/24, 29/29, 28/30, 22/30 weapons found a trajectory
  (v0.13.1: 6/26, 9/26). Eight strikes T21-T28, 14-21 hits each.
- **Defenses earlier:** 5 Kinetic at T15, 17 by T19, 20 at war; telescopes from T16 saw the human's defenses (12-16).
- **First AI strikes that dented the human:** population 3,306 -> 3,108 at T24.
- Repair 6-40 a turn; research queue bug (see below) scrambled the order but science was spent.

## Why it still lost (ranked)
1. **It never saw a human city.** Target view of jogi: `cities 0, cityPop 0` every turn. The human's counterintel was
   x2.05 from T15 (rising to x11.7 with 16 Data Security Hubs); Bolide's first Telescopes came at T16. Its Missiles had
   no city to aim at: our second-phase targeting picks visible cities or weapons, and only at T22 (8 strikes at 11
   visible Missile buildings) did it see anything; the rest fell to the native choice. 20-ish unintercepted Missile hits
   a turn (the human had 0 Ion) left the human's population growing.
2. **Its cities were not covered against Missiles in time.** At T22 it saw 11 human Missiles; it had 7-11 Ion covering
   2 of 5 cities. The human's first volley (T23, 14 Missiles, 4 intercepted) took population 2,740 -> 860 and 2 cities;
   T24-T28 finished it (460, 306, 45). Ion count rose to 29 only after the cities fell.
3. **Energy and material it could not use:** 5-9k energy unspent a turn from T20 (weapon firing cap 40% of income,
   then the 30-weapon cap), material stock 16-32k.
4. **Research queue bug (v0.14.1):** `AddTechnologyToQueueById(id, clearPrevious: true)` on each of three adds kept only
   the last target (T9-T11: 4th Gen Nuclear queued and wiped three times). Fixed in 0.14.2 (`6146699`), installed.
