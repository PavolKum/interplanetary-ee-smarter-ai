### Reading the results

- **I9 (the placement fix).** Land placements given up fell from 0.72 to 0.57 per AI-turn. This is one AI in a 1v1 on a
  different crust, so treat it as a first reading, not a proven effect.
  - 117 land searches: 100 placed, 17 gave up. The preference skipped 43,890 water anchors.
  - Placed in full: 17 railguns, 28 telescope arrays and 22 solar plants. In match 28 railguns (10) and telescope
    arrays (9) gave up.
  - **The 17 give-ups have two causes:**
    - 7 (4 nuclear, 3 solar plants) had only water anchors. These are plants the director queued for offshore mines
      left unpowered after the human's hits (T25-T27). The game's pool was kept, by design, and the plants could not
      reach the sea. **v0.22 candidate:** queue a floating plant when the unpowered buildings are on water.
    - 10 widened to every anchor after 60 steps and still found no spot: crowding on land.
  - Ion defenses gave up 20 times. They go through the cover search, which v0.21 does not change.
- **Covered buildings FAIL (0.749).** A first, so it needs a look before v0.22. Two plausible reasons, neither verified:
  - in a 1v1 nobody hit Tarkoy before T25, so its defenses started late (first at T15) and it held 0% cover at T14;
  - 20 ion defenses gave up while 64 offshore mines spread its value over open water.
  - The fix itself does not place defenses. It does put more non-defense buildings on the map, and those need covering.

## How it went

- Tarkoy matched the 2-AI build curve to T12, then went to 17 floating plants (T13) and 10.6k energy per turn by T19.
- War at T20: 14 railguns, all placed, then 13 missiles. Its energy reached 33.9k at T22.
- The human took 3 volleys at T22-T24 (18, 12 and 12 hits) and held steady (population 3,248 at T22, 3,142 at T23, 3,236 at T24).
- The human broke Tarkoy's power from T25: it repaired 28-31 buildings a turn, and its energy fell to 11.7k by T27.
- Tarkoy queued a nuclear missile at T27. The human's population fell from 3,576 to 3,312 at T30 (cause not confirmed).
- Tarkoy turned to defense (10 and 13 kinetic defenses at T28 and T30) and fell from 195 to 116 buildings by T30.
