### Diagnosis of the two FAILs (left as FAIL; changing a gate after the result is Pavol's call)

- **HUMAN-FIRST share.** 8 fire decisions without the flag:
  - **3** were attrition volleys whose commit *was* the human (Brain T27, Analog T41 and T44). The gate counts the
    `humanFirst` flag, not "commit = human".
  - **5** were Brain's return fire at Simmons (T21-T26), its attacker. That is v0.16.2 behaviour, but the v0.20 latch put
    Brain at war from T16 rather than T20, so it had more turns to return fire. Simmons (an original level-1 AI) built 19
    railguns by T17 and fired on Brain from T11.
  - **Proposed amendment:** count `commit == human` and report return fire separately.
- **Reserve shortfall.** 5 records, all small (1-583 energy), all in an AI's final collapse: Brain at T27 and T29 (106 to
  32 buildings), Analog at T32 (102), T42 and T44 (falling from 118 to 58 buildings). The reserve code did not change in
  v0.20.
  - **Proposed amendment:** exclude turns where the AI lost more than 20% of its buildings, or report them separately.

## How it went

- **Simmons attacked first.** It built 19 railguns by T17 and hit Brain from T11.
  - Brain's retaliation latched at T16, and it spent T21-T26 returning fire on Simmons.
  - The human then broke Brain (106 to 50 buildings at T27).
- **Analog went to war at T20 and was material-bound throughout:** 17-18k energy idle at T20-T24 with 3-5k material.
  - HUMAN-FIRST from T22. 96 of its paid shots went to the human, 41 to Simmons and 27 to Brain.
  - It peaked at 212 buildings and 30 guns (T35), queued a nuclear missile at T30, and bought population-damage
    upgrades.
  - Its aim fell late: 7-11 of 28-29 guns found a target at T36-T38, with 9-14 of them facing away.
- **The human was never in danger.**
  - One dip in population: 3,601 to 3,270 at T28. It peaked at 4,594 at T46.
  - Buildings went 72 (T20) to 177 (T34+).
  - His crust ran dry at T43.
- **Late free fire:** 1 late pulse volley (paid 25 = 23 + 2) and 6 refusals, all for energy. The cadence rule held in
  play.
- **Abort guard:** Analog's T25 strike was called off with 1 of 12 weapons on target (3 needed).
