## Observations (raw, before Terra)
1. **The wall did not stop the Railguns.** Human fire from T26: 30, 29, 49+35 misses, 46+50, 51+30, 84, 96 shots/turn
   (his weapons: 10 Railguns T21, 22 T24, 34 T27, 41 T30). Building HP destroyed per turn T26-30: 9.1k, 13.6k, 12.8k,
   8.2k, 4.1k, plus population 150-626M a turn, against 19-21 standing Kinetic Defenses (80% each against Railguns).
2. **Defense energy looks the binding constraint.** Each defense roll costs the defender 100 energy (Kinetic), failed
   rolls included. At ~2 shots per Railgun, 34-41 Railguns send 70-96 shots/turn, i.e. roughly 9-10k energy of rolls.
   Oort's reserve was 2.8-3.7k (capped at 30% of income, and sized from ~22 known weapons x 150). The human, for contrast,
   ended his turns with 15k energy left at T27 and 7-13k later. To check: interception outcomes are not in either log.
3. **Economy ceiling lower than v0.4.1** (163 vs 280 buildings at peak; 12k vs 25k E/t at T26). At T21-27 only 3.0-4.4k of
   10-12k E/t was spendable (`energyStart`): weapon upkeep 3.6-4.9k plus the 2.3-3.7k reserve took ~two thirds of income.
4. **The human had Kinetic only** (15-26 Kinetic, 0 Ion at every checkpoint). Oort fired Railguns almost exclusively
   (36 queued vs a few Missiles). Kinetic Defense does not stop Missiles in Enhanced; Ion does. Oort was blind to the
   human for much of the match (his counterintel 3.25 -> 8.3), so it could not see his defense mix.
5. **Nuclear Missile queued 5 times (T26, 27, 29, 33, 34), one placed, none observed firing**; placement gave up once.
   Land was full: 14 Solar Plant placements abandoned.
6. Fire quality: 271 shots planned as hits, 57 as misses; 90% of powered weapon-turns had a primary trajectory.

## Questions for the diagnosis review
- Is the reserve (cap 30% of income, ~150/weapon) far below the energy interceptions actually need? Does an exhausted
  reserve explain 21 defenses stopping little?
- Defense coverage: do 21 Kinetic Defenses (radius 5.9) cover a 160-building planet?
- Why no Missiles into a Kinetic-only wall, and could the AI have known (blind)?
- Did the defense spending cost more growth than it saved (economy ceiling)?
