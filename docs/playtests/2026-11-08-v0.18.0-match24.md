## Bugs, in v0.19 order

1. **"Aim search over 90 s" is mostly the human's own turn, not a delay (corrected after the match).** 19 of 67 searches
   logged more than 90 s (max 148 s at T32), most with no extension. But the native AI cannot become ready before every
   human has ended his turn (`AICore`: `m_timeOutTimer >= m_timeOut && PlayerManager.HaveAllHumanPlayersFinishedTurn`),
   so `seconds` measures until the later of the AI's window and the human's end turn. T1 took 116 s with no weapons:
   that was Pavol's first turn. The stop labels "hold window" and "cap" misreport these turns. v0.19 logs when the
   human finished, so the real extra wait can be measured.
2. **Fire held on stale energy (one-turn lag).** `Strategy.FreeEnergy` is `min(lastUnspent, lastUnspent + growth)`, so
   growth in this turn's income never counts. Doc at T23: free 1,034 against 3,500, with income up from 8.9k to 26k and
   17,120 left at the end of the turn; it fired the next turn. Eddie at T26 is the same (free 711, 17,990 left).
3. **Unaimed guns face away from the target:** 111 degrees on average (29 samples). Eddie aimed 6/18 at T22 and 4/13 at
   T30.
4. **Placement gave up** 78 times: IonDefense 26, SolarPlant 17, NuclearPlant 14, Railgun 8, KineticDefense 6,
   TelescopeArray 6, Mine 1.
5. **Idle stock:** Doc sat on 10-15k materials from T31 to T33.
