## Diagnosis
Two new findings, and the match 18 bottleneck confirmed.

### 1. The commit rule turns the last strong AI on the wounded ones
- **The rule** (`Strategy.Decide`): the commit value is `passing x (1 + LeaderInfluence x score / maxScore)`, with
  `LeaderInfluence` 0.5. The leader term is at most 1.5x, so an enemy whose defenses stop half the salvo loses to one
  whose defenses stop nothing.
- **T29:** Asona saw 2 human cities and still committed to Turing. Turing scored 18 through x 1.15 = 20.7 against the
  human's 10 x 1.5 = 15, which clears the stickiness bar (1.25 x 15 = 18.75).
- **From T31:** Brain (26 through, 2 of 31 intercepted) beat the human (13 through) the same way, and stickiness kept Asona
  on Brain until Brain died.
- **The human's Railguns made this happen:** they stripped the other AIs' defenses, which turned those AIs into Asona's best targets.
- **Return fire hits the wrong enemy.** `return-fire` (T30-T35) triggered on the human's impacts on Asona, but it fires
  at the commit target. Asona answered the human's volleys by shooting Brain.

### 2. The saturation gate cannot be met against a defended human
- **Timing:** match 18's sight window closed before any weapon existed. This time weapons and sight overlapped for 3
  turns (T22-T24), and the gate held all three AIs.
- **The cap:** with 30 weapons and a 0.47 pass share, ~14 through is the ceiling if every weapon aims. With 16-20 aiming,
  it was 7.5-9.
- **Result:** 12 through was out of reach against this human, so max-hold was the only path to firing, and by then the AIs fired blind.

### 3. Material starvation again
- **Unspent energy:** 74-81% of each AI's energy over the match, and 87-89% at T20-T26. Match 18 on the same measure:
  57-66% and 75-84%.
- **Peak unspent:** 39.7-41.1k at T24-T25.
- **Planets:** kept 55-69% of their material.

## v0.16.2 candidates (ranking updated from match 18)
1. **Material extraction to the war horizon** (match 18 #1, now confirmed).
2. **Commit rule: the human first while he leads.**
   - Scale the leader term so a non-leader AI wins only when the human cannot be reached (passing near 0), or forbid a
     commit to an AI while the human is visible and leading.
   - Aim return fire at whoever hit us.
   - This is the v0.9-v0.10 kingmaking failure, back through a different path.
3. **Strike while seen, with unguided weapons too.** When a human city is visible and weapons exist, fire below
   `MinPassingShots`, or treat the visible city as a precision target for Railguns. Matches 16, 18 and 19 each had a sight
   window that closed unused.
4. **Idle-energy science sink with a Jamming material carve-out** (match 18 #2).
5. **Defense geometry and Ion sites:** 34 of 48 searches gave up.
6. **Science Institute misses:** 4 of 6 AIs.
7. **Still deferred:** save/load persistence, forecast refinements.
