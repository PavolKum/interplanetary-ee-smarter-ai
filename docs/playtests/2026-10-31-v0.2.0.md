# Playtest 31 Oct 2026 - AI v0.2.0, 4 AIs (Normal) vs Pavol, human won at turn 41

Sources: `BepInEx/InterplanetaryAI/decisions-20261031-083702.jsonl` (AI), telemetry recording `37d7d80a-...jsonl` (human).
Pavol's verdict: "not much difference... still quite weak. They had weak economies."

## Bug found: energy savings froze construction (fixed in 0.2.2)
Energy does not carry over between turns (`PlayerResourceHandler.TurnStartInit`: `Energy = EnergyProductionPerTurn`).
The superweapon reserve saved energy anyway, so from turn 25 (Nuclear Missile unlocked) it took all usable energy
every turn. Building counts stayed flat or fell until each AI died; material piled up unused to 12-14k.
No superweapon was bought. Pino reached "buy pending" at T35-37 but hit the no-power-slot burst (Solar Plants 7 -> 14),
the in-game confirmation of Terra v0.2 finding 1 (fixed in 0.2.1).

Pavol's build order: mines first (6 by T6, 10 by T12), then Floating Plants (83) and Offshore Mines (27),

AIs: 3 Solar Plants on T2, 1-3 Mines ever, Railguns from T6-9, firing upkeep eats most energy income
(usable energy 0-800 of 1.5-3k per turn). They spent their railguns on each other (Mitnick/Oort 177 weapon-turns)
and into Pavol's Kinetic Defense, which counters railguns.
The planner logged 120 successful "material" picks yet the AIs own 1-3 mines: a material pick with no
free power slot only raises PowerSlotNeed, and native then builds a power plant (to verify).
