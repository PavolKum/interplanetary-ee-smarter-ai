# Playtest 8 Nov 2026 (twenty-fifth match) - AI v0.19.0: Zenon, Donnels and Sarai (Smarter AI, Impossible) vs Beastie (original, level 1) vs Pavol: the AI won at turn 38

**First Smarter AI win.** After 24 human wins, three Smarter AIs on v0.19.0 eliminated Pavol on turn 38. This was the
first lobby with three Eiais. The crust was 400,000. Claude monitored it live from T1.

**Caveat: the human played an experimental nuclear-weapons-only offense** ("I went with this weird strategy of only
nuclear weapons and... Yeah, that was a bad choice, I guess"). Each launch was expensive, which explains the T32
over-commit that starved his defense rolls. The nukes explain the 25-40 repairs a turn on the AIs. Without conventional
fire he could not grind one AI down. So this win measures the AI against an off-meta human strategy, not yet against
his standard play.

Pavol restarted once at T1: a first lobby (Helios, Uuno, Ruffian, Nanto) appears in the same decision file with one turn
each and is ignored here.

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v019m25-*` (git-ignored). Mod 0.19.0, 16/16 hooks, turn guard 9/9, aim watch 1/1.

- **Result:** human eliminated at T38. All three Eiais and Beastie were alive at their last records (T37).
- **Peak energy per turn:** Zenon 52.0k (T34), Donnels 48.9k (T34), Sarai 41.4k (T30); the human 20.5k (T29). The AIs
  had about 2.5x his peak.

## How the AIs won

- **Weapons came late but all at once.**
  - The research plan pushed the war turn to T20, not the configured T16 (Orbital Assembly and Maglev first).
  - Until then all three built economy, about 20 ion defenses each and 8 telescope arrays each.
  - At T20 they queued 52 railguns together and fired from T21.
- **All three chose the human on 49 of 51 commits** (corrected by the v0.20 council): HUMAN-FIRST from T21 to T37,
  except Zenon and Sarai on T23, who switched to Beastie. About a third of all paid trajectories were not recorded
  at the human (fellow Eiais, own/neutral bodies, misses).
- **Volleys at the human:**

  | AI | Turns | Hits | Misses |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Zenon | 14 | 262 | 59 |
  | Donnels | 13 | 253 | 60 |
  | Sarai | 13 | 259 | 60 |
  | Total | | 774 | 179 |

- **Aim held up:** most firing turns found trajectories for 15-27 of 22-31 guns.
- **Projectile and population upgrades:** Sarai and Donnels bought RailgunProjectileAmount and RailgunPopulationDamage.
  Expected shots through the human's defenses rose from about 7 per AI at T21 to 30-45 by T33-T37.
- **The human's defense economy broke first.**
  - At T32 he spent most of his energy on his own volley and left too little for defense rolls ("I overcook it when
    firing out"). Population fell from 2,132 to 1,392, then to 677 at T33.
  - Even then three AIs firing about 85-90 shots a turn needed more energy to intercept than he had ("it just takes
    more energy to defend than I have").
  - Population went from 2,589 (T20) to 266 (T37).
- **Buildings never fell:** 73 at T21, 210 at T37. The damage went to population, as RailgunPopulationDamage intends.
- **The human hit back hard.** Zenon, Donnels and Sarai each repaired 25-40 buildings a turn from T31. Zenon's covered
  city share fell to 80% at T36. But none of them broke.
- **Four nuclear missiles were queued:** Sarai T26, Donnels T31, Zenon T31 and T34. Whether any landed is not visible in
  these logs.

## v0.19 changes in play

- **Late free fire works and pays.**
  - Zenon fired late 6 times (T25-T28, T31, T32), with 4,303-6,676 energy left against 2,800-3,900 reservations.
  - Every late volley was paid: shots paid = firing hits + misses (25, 17, 25, 23, 27, 28; corrected from "planned"). No aborts.
  - Late volleys keep the native 20 s aim window: T26 aimed 11 of 30 guns.
- **The aim extension's real cost is now measured.**
  - Per turn, the longest AI wait after the human ended his turn was 0-25 s, 10 s on average, 186 s over 18 war turns.
  - On 7 of 18 turns it was 0.
  - The old "aim times" (up to 191 s here at T30) were the human's own turn.

## Still broken

- **Placement gave up** 107 times (corrected from 127; the breakdown already summed to 107): NuclearPlant 52 (Sarai 33), SolarPlant 32, IonDefense 22, Coilcannon 1.
- **Unaimed guns face away:** 99 degrees on average (51 samples). Retargeting is the worst case: at T23 Zenon and Sarai
  switched to Beastie and aimed 3 of 30 and 6 of 29.
- **Idle stock:** Sarai held 29-38k materials at T35-T36 (keepM 15,000); Donnels had 19-30k energy unspent at T23-T28.

## v0.20 candidates

- **Weapon placement facing the likely target**, still first. Every retarget wastes most of a volley.
- **Placement failures for power plants** (now the largest class).
- **Do not over-tune toward the human now.** This was one match, with a three-Eiai lobby and a human mistake at T32.
  Play more three-AI and two-AI lobbies on v0.19.0 before changing the balance.
