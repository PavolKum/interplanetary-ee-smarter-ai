# Playtest 31 Oct 2026 (eleventh match) - AI v0.11.1, two AIs (Beam, Brother, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 47

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0111-*.jsonl` (git-ignored), live monitor.

## What worked
- Same opening as v0.10.1, turn for turn (deterministic): parity at T12, AI lead from T13. 10k+ energy per turn by
  T19; peak 61.5k (Beam T29) and 55.8k (Brother T28) vs the human's 17.8-23.7k at the same turns.
- Global material project (Experimental Matter Control at #10) from T16; Reactor Trials from T21.
- Defenses: 40-60% of impacts stopped (Beam T27 26 impacts / 15 stopped; T29 37 / 19; Brother T29 30 / 11) vs
  v0.10.1, where 70-98% of impacts met no eligible defense.
- First strikes went at the human (T21: both AIs; the human showed 0 visible defenses). Longest match so far (T47).
- The abort gate works as coded (first live verification) - but see 3: the rule is harmful as designed.

## Why it still lost
1. **Planet material exhausted (decisive).** Every planet starts with 350k material. Brother's ran out at T26, Beam's
   at T27 (104k/124k left at T24). Mining ran at 30-45k a turn; the last stock went on ~180 Floating Plants each
   (energy that could not be spent). From T28 both AIs had ~30-135 material at construction: no building, repair,
   defense or weapon replacement ("nothing" every turn, 17-40k energy unspent) while being worn down
   (Beam 446 -> 8 buildings T28-T45, Brother 504 -> eliminated T45). The human ran dry at T38, eleven turns later,
   with the game decided. v0.11's material push (global project, glut pricing) accelerated extraction. The v0.4 endgame
   bank only runs while superweapon research is queued, so it never engaged.
2. **Few weapons find a trajectory, especially at the human.** Weapons with a primary trajectory per fire record:
   537 of 1,478 live powered weapons over the match (36%; Sol v0.12 corrected the first draft's 21%, which divided by
   2,568 retained targeting entries, destroyed weapons included). Against the human mostly 6-15 of 25-54 (once 36 of 53); against the other AI
   14-32 of ~53. Native code (`AICore`, `AIWeaponTargeting`): trajectory search runs per frame only during the human's
   turn; the AI is ready once 20 s have passed (`m_timeOut`) and the human has ended the turn; per-weapon step
   allowance about clamp(1200 / total AI weapons, 30, 100) per frame, main thread. Sol v0.12: 18 of 26 strike
   decisions came on human turns already longer than 40 s, so wall time is not the main limit; work per frame
   (the 30-step floor) and geometry (distance, orbit position) are. Extra weapons add upkeep faster than damage.
   Caveat: the `weapons` field in `fire` records stays at 53-54 after weapons are destroyed (stale targetable entries).
3. **Abort gate threw away aimed weapons.** 26 fire decisions, 13 aborted (Brother 9, Beam 4), threshold 30% of
   weapons on target. In energy glut the saved firing energy was worthless; 6-15 aimed weapons would have hit.
4. **Target flip-flop.** Commit alternated human / other AI almost every turn T21-T34 (decisions: Beam -> human 7,
   -> Brother 3; Brother -> human 9, -> Beam 7). Cause: `ReachabilityMemory` marks a target unreachable for every weapon
   that found no trajectory - designed for geometry, but most failures are search time. After aiming at X, ~40 weapons
   mark X unreachable, so next turn the other target looks better. CommitStickiness 1.25 cannot hold against that.
5. **Executed strikes:** at the human T21 (16 shots), T23 (19), T29 (41), T36/T42 (partial, a few shots each);
   AI vs AI T26, T28 (both), T30, T32, T34, T36. Most landed damage went AI vs AI. The human kept growing
   throughout (58 -> 190 buildings, 3,088 -> 5,000 population T21-T44).

## Next (v0.12)
1. **Material budget over the whole game.** Plan extraction against planet material left; do not run dry before the
   war is decided (the human: T38); keep a reserve for repair, defense and weapons; stop buying power while energy goes
   unspent; make the endgame bank independent of superweapon research.
2. **Fire what can aim.** Replace the all-or-nothing abort: fire weapons that have a trajectory, hold the rest.
3. **Reachability memory:** count only searches that finished without a trajectory, or ignore the memory while the
   found share is low against every target. Add stickiness on a commit that has a trajectory record.
4. **Cap weapons near what the search serves** (~15-25 per AI); spend surplus on per-weapon upgrades (projectiles,
   building damage).
5. (Pavol's call) longer AI aiming window: raise the 20 s minimum; costs waiting time between turns.
6. Target by threat (who hits us, who is winning: cities, population), not visible economy.
7. Log the `weapons` count from live weapons, not targetable entries.
