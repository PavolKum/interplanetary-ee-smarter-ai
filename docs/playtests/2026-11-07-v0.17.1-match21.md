## What lost it
1. **Aiming.** On firing turns Corona often found trajectories for few of its live weapons:

   | Turn | Weapons with a trajectory | Out of |
   |---|---|---|
   | T45 | 13 | 32 |
   | T50 | 13 | 27 |
   | T52 | 4 | 28 |
   | T53 | 9 | 31 |
   | T56 | 4 | 28 |

   - On those turns no weapon was marked unreachable, so the search ran out of time.
   - Against Shoe (T38-T44) 26-32 of 32 aimed.
   - So the human's planet is the hard target. More aim time (or more steps) against a hard target is the cheapest big gain.
   - On other turns 5-12 weapons were truly unreachable from where they stood (weapon placement).
2. **Hesitation.**
   - War started at T20 (Orbital Assembly was not ready for T16).
   - Corona held at T21-T25 below the 12-shot gate (4.7-8.5 through against 19-25 defenses).
   - Free fire never triggered: 17-1,918 energy free against 2,000-3,680 cost. The defense reserve and the firing hold took the surplus.
   - The first volley came on max-hold at T26.
3. **Mining the planet dry.**
   - 119 Offshore Mines at T34; the planet went from 400k to 0 by T39.
   - The 116k stockpile then went on repairs (up to 7k a turn) until material ran out (3.3k at T56, income -2.1k).
   - The drain guard (horizon T36) did not hold back the late mine rush (MBOUND/XFIRST bought 9-23 mines a turn at T28-T34).
4. **Volleys at the wrong enemy.**
   - T37: precision strike on Traitor.
   - T38-T39 and T43-T44: return fire on Shoe.
   - All while 27-28 shots per volley could have reached the human.
   - Return fire and precision come before human-first in `Strategy.Decide`.
5. **Idle surplus.** 15-21k energy unspent per turn from T34 to T46, at the 30-weapon cap, with nothing to buy.

## v0.18 candidates
1. **Aim time:** a longer aiming window or more steps when the targeting search times out (aimed far below live with no unreachable mark). Measure from `aim-budget` and the fire records.
2. **Human first over return fire and precision:** keep return fire for when the human is out of reach (passing below the floor).
3. **Hold less:** free fire should count the released firing reservation; or lower the gate when the human's defenses grow faster than the AI's weapons (T21-T25).
4. **Planet budget in war:** extraction-first and mine buying respect the drain guard after the war starts; keep a reserve for repairs.
5. **Weapon placement:** place weapons where trajectories to the committed target exist. The unreachable share was 5-12 of 30, depending on the target planet.
6. **Surplus:** a sink for energy above the cap (defense upgrades, intel, superweapon repeats) instead of 20k idle.
7. **City ring:** the Ion ring still gives up half the time.
