# Playtest 31 Oct 2026 (sixth match) - AI v0.7.3, two AIs (Ash, Colossus, level 1) vs Pavol: human Victorious at turn 41

Sources: `decisions-20261031-161404.jsonl` (444 records), telemetry `b8c08ca4-...`, live monitor.
Data in `docs/playtests/data/v073-*.jsonl` (git-ignored). Longest match so far (v0.4.1 T38, v0.5.0 T36, v0.6.0 T37).

## What changed
- **The AIs' economies never collapsed.** Peak ~188 buildings each (T31), 13-15k E/t; Colossus still had 174 buildings,
  14k E/t and 11.7k M/t at T40, more buildings than the human (172). In v0.6.0 both AIs fell apart from T28/T32.
- **Coverage-aware placement ran without errors** (0 placement failures for defenses, no power-line warnings). Kinetic
  coverage by value: 25% with 3 defenses, 44-56% with 9, then a plateau of 50-71% (Colossus) and 40-56% (Ash, whose
  building count outgrew its defenses). Colossus kept 4-5 of 5 cities covered until T33.
- Water-plant fallback visibly ran (Solar Plant give-ups followed by Floating Plants), not yet confirmed by queued roles.

## How the human won: no conventional weapons, then Nuclear Missiles
The human built **no Railguns at all**: economy, 22-28 Kinetic + 5 Ion Defenses, 3-7 Data Security Hubs, then
**9 Nuclear Missiles at T32, 17 at T36**. Shots: 24 nuclear shots at T30-34, 48 at T35-39. Damage per turn from T32:
11-24k building HP and 450-1,350 M population. The AIs lost cities, not economy: Ash 5 -> 1 city (T32-T36), eliminated
T37; Colossus 5 -> 1 (T33-T38), defeated T41 with its economy intact. Defeat = every city at 0 population.

Until T32 every impact the AIs logged came from each other: weapon-turns at the human 523/756 (Ash/Colossus), at each
other 400/382, i.e. a third of their fire went into the other AI while the human, unarmed, sat behind 27+ Kinetic.

## v0.8 candidates
1. **Anti-nuke city defense.** Nuclear Missiles are stopped by Ion (0.8, Enhanced), and they kill cities. When an enemy
   Nuclear Missile is seen or one hits us (ledger by weapon), build Ion Defenses placed to cover cities (coverage model per
   defense type with city weight high for Ion), and consider city projects (City Barrier/DDI). Check what the AIs saw of the
   human's 9-17 Nuclear Missiles (counterintel) and whether their own Nuclear Missiles (bought T27/T28) fired.
2. **Don't fight the other AI while the human outgrows both.** A third of the fire went AI-vs-AI; the leader term should
   weigh the human's visible/estimated economy and superweapon threat more.
3. Coverage keeps pace with growth: Ash's coverage fell as buildings outgrew defenses (floor 15% of buildings).
