# Playtest 1 Nov 2026 (sixteenth match) - AI v0.15.3, 1v1 (Labo, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 33

Same map type, 1v1, live monitor. Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0153-*` (git-ignored). Mod loaded clean (0.15.3,
15/15 hooks); the only exception is the console's CSharpCodeCompiler mono path. **`init.shadows` = false**: the match
ran with SeenBuildingsShadows off, so a city that stops being Visible is forgotten (Sol v0.15.2 #7 confirmed).

## The human's plan (the usual build-out)
Pure economy to T22: energy 1,800 (T12), 3,310 (T16), 7,215 (T20), 29,410 (T25). Counterintel first: 3 Data Security
Hubs at T12 (x2.05), 11 at T20 (x5.35), 18-21 from T22 (x16-27). 5 Kinetic at T13, 20 at T20, 30 from T24; no Ion.
Weapons late and all at once: 20 Railguns at T23, 35 at T24, 40 at T26, 21 Lasers at T30. Volleys of 105-120 shots
(T26-T29).

## What v0.15.3 changed, measured
- **Weapon identity fix works:** `accuracy.live` equals the strategy's weapon count every turn (9, 13, ... 24, 7, 4);
  `stale` 0 until the T27 losses, then 18-21 stale entries removed. Guided 9-15, planned rate 0.80-0.81.
- **Return fire fired once, correctly:** T26, the turn after the human's first impacts (21). Before that it held T21-T23
  with 90-100% expected through, not under attack (the human had no weapons until T23).
- **Opening reserve worked from T8:** one counterintel project instead of two and 2 Floating Plants a turn earlier than
  Thistle. Energy per turn 805 (T8), 1,205 (T10), 1,905 (T12), 4,625 (T16), 7,265 (T20): ahead of Thistle, behind
  v0.14.1 Bolide (17,740 at T20). T6-T7 still bought 3 Telescopes plus material projects and no plant: the economy step
  prefers material purchases over plants while energy is the scarce resource.
- **City cover failed on placement, now measured:** the prior target (5 Ions) applied from T24. 28 `cover-search`
  records: 1 placed (T30, after 792 tries), 27 gave up. Almost every candidate failed `TryPlaceBuilding` ("unplaced":
  754 of 792 at T30, 1,800 of 1,800 at T31) with 9-15 anchors in reach and the ring geometry fine. The ring 3.1-4.25
  around each city was already built over by T24 (dense base, 150-218 buildings), and later also ruins.
  Logging bug: the give-up record repeats every step after the cap (15 copies at T31).

## Why it lost (ranked)
1. **Every one of its 45 Missile strikes went to the native random point** (`native-no-candidates`). The human's
   counterintel (x16-27) hid all five cities from T23 despite 208 intel, and with shadows off nothing was remembered
   from T8-T22, when it saw all five. 14-15 Missiles a volley against a human with no Ion would have landed on cities.
2. **The intel race cost it the economy:** 30 Telescopes by T26 (2 a turn while any city was unseen), about 6,000 energy
   to build plus 600 a turn upkeep, against multiplicative counterintel it could not outgrow.
3. **Economy half the human's at war:** 12,816 vs 29,410 energy at T25. Weapon purchases are capped by firing upkeep
   (0.4 x income), so the arsenal grew 2-4 a turn (24 at T26) while the human built 35 Railguns in two turns. Material
   stock 15-47k unused from T19.
4. **The human's first volley (T26, 105 Railguns) broke it:** 83 impacts (23 intercepted by 37-42 Kinetic), 2 cities
   and 17 of 24 weapons gone; buildings 218 -> 114 -> 40 by T29.

## v0.16 candidates
1. **Remember where cities were seen** (cities never move): candidates from genuine past sightings when the city is no
   longer Visible. Needs Pavol's call on fairness with shadows off.
2. **Stop the Telescope race** when more intel no longer reveals cities (counterintel growing faster); cap intel
   spending by its marginal result.
3. **City cover before the ring fills:** reserve or build the per-city Ions early (prior turn 24 -> ~14), or clear
   ruins/space on the ring; dedupe the give-up log.
4. **Energy-scarce opening:** value power plants above material projects while energy is the binding resource.
5. **Late material recovery** (Sol v0.15.2 #5) and the war weapon cap against a richer opponent.
