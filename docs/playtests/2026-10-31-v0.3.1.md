## Why they still lost
1. **They fought each other, not the human.** Weapon-turns aimed at the human: Am 242 of 4,233, Core 395 of 3,387,
   Shiver 125 of 3,155, Rebel 26 of 1,251 (~6%; native uniform random would be ~25%). LeaderWeight scores the
   *visible* economy: each AI saw the other AIs' huge economies and the human's small/hidden one. Smart targeting
   plus a boom turned the AIs into each other's main threat. Rebel died T33, Shiver T41, Am T42, Core T43.
2. **Planet material ran out at turns 26-28** (Am 340k at T15 -> 0 at T26). Yield upgrades on mines also multiply the
   planet drain. After that: material 0-72, no rebuilding, no purchases ("nothing" queued from T28), 15-60k energy idle.
3. **Weak science, no superweapon.** AI science 450-680/turn vs human 600-1,300; Thermonuclear Warheads never
   finished. Am sat on 78k material at T24 with nothing to spend it on but Railguns and plants.
4. **Railguns only** (no missile/coilcannon tech on the plan early); the human's Kinetic Defenses fit them.
5. **Placement loop:** a full planet made Railgun searches give up, refund and requeue: 241 (Core) and 196 (Shiver)
   abandoned placements. The weapon picker ignores failed types (`TryWeapon` skips no `FailedThisTurn` ids).
6. Energy-for-defense: spent to ~0 until T26, then large idle surpluses by accident (cap hit, no material).

## v0.4 plan (ranked)
1. Targeting: score threat by what an enemy can do to us and by who is winning the game, not raw visible economy;
   count the human's hidden strength (intel gaps) and give HumanBias meaning; avoid AI-vs-AI wars of attrition.
2. Planet-material budget: Multi-Stage Refinery (drain x0.7) when the reserve is short, bank material for the
   endgame (superweapons) before depletion, stop buying plants whose energy cannot be spent.
3. Science: Science Institute projects, population growth (city connectors, defense cover), research order with
   weapon tech (missiles) and Thermonuclear earlier.
4. Fix the placement loop (skip failed types in TryWeapon; treat repeated failures as "planet full").
5. Defense energy reserve from war onward (defense rolls cost the defender's leftover energy).
6. Late game when land is full: superweapons, weapon upgrades, replace instead of add.
