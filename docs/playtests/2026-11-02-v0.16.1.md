## Diagnosis
**v0.16.1 fixed the tech tempo and exposed the real bottleneck: material.**
- **Material-starved with a full planet.** All three AIs ended almost every turn with under 300 material left, while
  their planets kept 65-70% of their material to the end. `sustainableMaterial` was 13.6k a turn at T20, against an
  actual 5-9k. The human ran 1.4 material per energy through T20 and mined his planet dry by T29.
- **Energy without a use.** Over the match, 57-66% of each AI's energy went unspent (T20-T26: 75-84%). Each AI left
  22-27k unspent on its peak turns. SSTARs multiplied energy x2.86 while material stayed the limit, so most of the
  multiplier was wasted. Energy does not carry over.
- **No science sink.** Jamming needs 900 material, so the material shortage also blocked the science plan
  (0 Jammings; science a third of the human's).
- **Sight came early, weapons late.** The only sight window (T17-T18) preceded the first weapon (T20). After T19 the
  human's counterintel outran 16 arrays, and every volley was blind artillery.

## v0.16.2 candidates (ranked by evidence)
1. **Material extraction to the war horizon.** Treat material, not energy, as the binding resource when energy is
   idle and material ends the turn near zero: buy Mines and Offshore Mines first, up to the planet guard's horizon. The
   guard never bound (13.6k sustainable against 5-9k actual). Check against match 10's lesson (planets dry by T27)
   by holding `PlanetGuardHorizonTurn` at 36.
2. **The idle-energy science sink (from match 17), with a material carve-out for Jamming.** Once item 1 frees material,
   reserve 900 material per wanted Jamming after EMC so the reservation turns into purchases.
3. **Strike while seen.** When a human city is visible and no weapon exists yet, start the war early (sight alone, without
   waiting for Orbital Assembly). Guided weapons would then still have to pass the per-class quotas and fairness rules.
   Match 18's window was T17-T18.
4. **Defense geometry and Ion sites (deferred since v0.16).** 41-72% of impacts in qualifying volleys met no defense in
   range; 19 of 31 Ion cover searches gave up.
5. **Cruncher's missing Science Institute.** Trace why one of three identical AIs never bought it.
6. **Still deferred:** save/load persistence of the intel, war and volley state; forecast refinements (Sol v0.16 M2/M4/M6).
