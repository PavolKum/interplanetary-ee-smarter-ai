## First measured defense outcomes (the point of v0.6)
New hook on `PlayerDefenseHandler.IsHitIntercepted` works (14/14 hooks, no mod errors). Per AI turn it reports shots
that reached the planet in the previous action phase (`lastImpacts`), how many were intercepted, how many landed with
**no defense in range** (`lastUncovered`), rolls that found no energy (`lastNoEnergy`) and energy spent on rolls.

| Turn | AI | Defenses | Impacts | Intercepted | Uncovered | No-energy rolls | Energy spent | Reserve |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | X | 21 | 45 | 19 | 24 | 0 | 2,100 | 2,842 |
| 30 | X | 35 | 36 | 16 | 19 | 0 | 1,900 | 2,584 |
| 32 | Turing | 24 | 88 | 22 | 64 | 0 | 2,800 | 3,500 |
| 33 | Turing | 15 | 95 | 19 | 75 | 0 | 2,100 | 2,138 |
| 33 | X | 24 | 74 | 12 | 39 | **29** | 1,700 | 3,061 |
| 36 | X | 15 | 62 | 11 | 47 | 4 | 1,100 | 799 |

1. **Coverage, not energy, was the limit.** 53-85% of impacts landed where no defense was in range. When a defense was in
   range it stopped ~90% (T28 X: 19 of 21 covered; T32 Turing: 22 of 24). 21-40 Kinetic Defenses sit in a cluster that
   covers a fraction of the planet (radius 5.9 each on a ~19.6 planet: 48% at best with no overlap).
2. Energy ran short once (T33 X: 29 unpaid rolls, need 5,750 vs reserve 3,061), as its income and defenses collapsed.
   v0.5's "reserve too small" guess is mostly refuted; the observed reserve term is a secondary safeguard.
3. The AIs' own Railguns met the human's wall: observed pass fell from 1.00 (T18) to 0.23-0.54 as he built Kinetic
   Defenses. Missiles started appearing (X 2, Turing 1 at T29) once the pass estimate dropped: the weapon pick follows
   the measured rate. Too few to matter; the Railgun stock was already built.
4. Economy: with two AIs on one planet each, they matched each other turn for turn (identical to T12) and peaked
   at ~150-179 buildings, 10-12k E/t (T28), against the human's 171 buildings and 30k E/t. Defense building did not
   starve growth as badly as v0.5 suggested: both were growing until the human's volley (T28 X, T32 Turing).
5. Human: 220 buildings, 34k E/t, planet material ran out at T36 (income 0).
