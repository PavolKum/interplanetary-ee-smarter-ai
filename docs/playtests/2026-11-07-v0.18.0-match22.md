# Playtest 7 Nov 2026 (twenty-second match) - AI v0.18.0: Eiai (Smarter AI, Impossible) vs Gear and Turing (original, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 38

This was the first match on v0.18.0, which added the aim watch: on a firing turn, the search for trajectories keeps
going while it is still finding them, up to a cap. The crust was 350,000, smaller than match 21's 400,000.

Pavol played with Claude as an "intelligence chief" on a live voice call (two calls, 121 turns in the second). The
briefings used only his own telemetry, through `AI/tools/turn_brief.py`. Eiai's decisions and the monitor log were not
read out during play.

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v018m22-*` (git-ignored). Mod 0.18.0, 16/16 hooks, turn guard 9/9, aim watch 1/1.
Story video: `docs/strategy/video-match22/out/fortress-match22.mp4` (git-ignored).

- **Eliminations (last record):** Gear T36, Eiai T36, Turing T37. Human victory at T38.
- **Peak energy per turn:** Eiai 25.1k at T29; the human 39.8k at T33.

## The human built a fortress and lost nothing

- **Zero buildings lost all game.** The only population dip was about 80M, at T32-T33 (4,116 to 4,039), recovered the
  next turn.
- **40 defenses by T26**, earlier and more than match 21's 37 by T29. The whole planet was covered except the open ocean.
- **Economy first:** materials rose from 200 to 13,182 a turn by T24. The crust ran dry at T37, with a repair fund of
  31,757 banked.
- **Pavol's guns came late but heavy:** 34 railguns and 18 lasers by T33. That turn 89 of 118 shots hit a planet, for
  13,144 damage.

## v0.18 aim watch: works, but fire still did nothing

- **Firing turns aimed most guns:** T22 15/16, T24 12/13, T26 13/16, T30 11/20, T31 11/16, T32 16/22 (found/live). Match
  21 was 4-13 of about 30.
- **Extensions:** +20 to +50 s. Stops were "enough", "stall budget" or "cap"; hold turns stopped with "hold window".
- **HUMAN-FIRST from T21 to the end.** 6 volleys at the human counted 81 hits and 10 misses. No building fell.
- **Nuclear missile queued at T32 and T36.** Neither mattered.

## What lost it

- **The fortress came first.** Six volleys from 13-22 guns could not get through 40+ kinetic defenses. To win, Eiai has
  to strike before the fortress exists (before about T24).
- **Unaimed guns face away from the target:** the unfound weapons faced 120 degrees away on average (19 samples), so a
  longer search cannot find them. This is a placement problem.
- **Fire held with energy left over** on 4 turns (T21, T23, T25, T29). At T29 the hold said 1,083 free against a 3,030
  firing cost while 17,349 energy was left at the end of the turn. See match 24.
- **Placement gave up** 24 times: IonDefense 15, Railgun 5, and single Missile, DataSecurityHub, Solar and Nuclear plants.

## Open questions

- **Aim time over 90 s on 11 turns** (max 158 s at T1, with no weapons). Only one of them was an extension at the cap
  (T32, 100 s). The native AI cannot be ready before the human ends his turn, so the rest is the human's turn length,
  not a delay the mod adds (see match 24).
- One Error/Exception line in the BepInEx log, not yet inspected (probably the game's own XmlSerializer at startup).
- Eiai's T37 economy record is missing.

## v0.19 candidates

- Place weapons facing their likely target.
- Strike before the fortress is built.
- Fire decision on end-of-turn energy (see match 24).
- Log when the human ended his turn, so the aim extension's real extra wait is measured.
