# Playtest 31 Oct 2026 (tenth match) - AI v0.10.1, two AIs (Maverick, Clowrus, level 1) vs Pavol: human won at turn 39

Data: `docs/playtests/data/v0101-*.jsonl` (git-ignored), live monitor.

## The economy worked, for the first time by a wide margin
- Research on plan: 4th Gen Reactors at T9, Nuclear Plants from T10, first Reactor Trials T13 (Clowrus), 2-3 at once by T20-21.
- Parity at T12 (AIs 1,975-2,075 E/t, 3,188-3,357 M/t vs human 1,975 / 3,213); AI lead from T13.
- Energy per turn, AI best vs human: T15 4,388 vs 2,705; T17 5,766 vs 2,616; T21 22,545 vs 8,966; T23 52,809 vs 9,516;
  T26 65,719 vs 11,066; T30 67,096 vs 23,447. v0.9.1 AIs stalled at 6-7k.
- Material glut pricing fired at T19: 14-19 Nuclear Plants queued per AI in one turn.
- Hold fire released 3-18k energy per hold turn into construction.

## Why it still lost: the energy had nowhere to go
1. **Material, then planet space, became binding.** Clowrus T21: 19.8k energy budget, material 14.6k -> 115, 11.5k energy
   unspent. From T22 both AIs left 8-59k energy unspent every turn. Maverick's planet filled at ~T24: 22-55 placements a
   turn abandoned (T28: 55 Railguns tried, 1 placed). Unspent energy only funds defense rolls (no unpaid roll all match).
2. **Kingmaking.** Commit = max(leader score x expected shots through). The other AI's visible economy (energy + material,
   ~21k at T21) outscored the human's (13.5k, low material), so 6 of the first 6 strikes (T22-T30) were AI vs AI, every one
   while the model rated the human the softer target (e.g. T26 Clowrus: 33 through at the human vs 19 at Maverick).
   Clowrus 285 -> 175 buildings by T31 mostly from Maverick.
3. **Strikes at the human did nothing measurable.** T30 Maverick 47 shots (~20 predicted through), T32 45 (~20), T33 Clowrus
   25 (~15): the human's population and building count never dropped (pop 4,328 -> 4,726, 156 -> 178 buildings).
   The salvo model is badly optimistic against the human (26 Kinetic + 5 Ion); the observed correction swung (Maverick's
   estimate 20 -> 4 -> 20 at T30-32). The decision log does not record our own impacts per target, so it cannot be calibrated yet.
4. **Human fire volume.** 37 Railguns with Railgun Projectile Amount upgrades = 111 shots a turn from T30 (+10-14 Lasers),
   every turn (no pulsing this time). AI defenses: 70-98% of impacts met no eligible defense (Maverick T33: 85 impacts,
   83 no eligible defense, 1 intercepted; 284 -> 212 -> 138 buildings). The AIs never built a Shield against the Lasers.
5. Max-share gate too strict on big salvos: Clowrus T29 held with ~40 predicted through (44% < 45%).

## Next (v0.11)
1. Commit by expected effect, not leader score: weight softness (shots through) at least as much as economy; never ignore
   a much softer third player; don't attack whoever is already being hit by someone else unless finishing.
2. Log our own impacts / intercepted per target each turn (the ledger has it) and calibrate the salvo model on it;
   fire when passing >= 2 x MinPassingShots regardless of share.
3. Space is the scarce resource once energy is in glut: weapon upgrades by damage per building (projectile amount),
   stop re-trying placements on a full planet, spend surplus energy on material multipliers and upgrades.
4. Defense coverage against 100+ shot turns: no-eligible dominates, not energy; Shields when Lasers are seen.
